Getting here

Notes from researching the Delhi–Limerick run for 30 August 2026. Fares move constantly — treat the numbers as a snapshot, not a quote.

Fly into Dublin, not Shannon Shannon looks closer, but its only real feed is Heathrow on Aer Lingus, 2–3 flights a day — and the direct Shannon→UL shuttle runs only 06:00–18:00. Dublin has coaches to campus around the clock, hourly, so your arrival time stops being a constraint. There is no direct Delhi–Dublin flight; everything is one stop.

Check baggage at the airline, never the aggregator Google Flights reports weight-based allowances as piece counts and gets Gulf carriers wrong — it showed "2 free checked bags" for a fare that is actually 30 kg by weight. Open the fare rules inside the airline's own booking flow before you compare prices. Two verified examples for 30 Aug 2026: Etihad 30 kg checked + 7 kg cabin; SWISS 1 bag × 23 kg + 8 kg cabin, with a second SWISS bag from ₹20,033.
Arrival weekend is a price peak Both Etihad's and SWISS's own fare calendars showed 30 August priced well above the days either side — 27–28 August ran roughly ₹20,000–25,000 cheaper. That is the whole cohort flying at once. Flying earlier only helps if your accommodation licence has already started, otherwise hotel nights eat the saving.

Transit visas — Indian passport holders

  • UK (Heathrow): normally a Direct Airside Transit Visa is required, but Home Office guidance TWOV 3(j) exempts anyone "travelling on to the Republic of Ireland" with a valid Irish biometric visa. Carry a printout — check-in staff get this wrong.
  • Germany, France, Netherlands: all dropped the airport transit visa for Indian passports during 2026.
  • Abu Dhabi, Dubai, Doha, Istanbul: never needed one for airside transit. Gulf routings avoid the question entirely.

Packing & customs

Dublin immigration comes before baggage claim, so every document travels in your cabin bag. The other rule that catches Indian students: no dairy, no meat, no fresh produce.

The classic seizure Ghee and milk sweets. Dairy and meat from non-EU countries cannot be brought into Ireland at all — not sealed, not vacuum-packed, not "just a little for home". Items are destroyed, not returned, and undeclared goods carry penalties. Limerick has Indian grocery stores; buy it there.

Medicines

  • Original packaging with the pharmacy label. Do not decant tablets into unlabelled boxes.
  • Prescription in your own name — without it, prescription medicine is treated as restricted.
  • Doctor's letter listing conditions and generic drug names. Indian brand names mean nothing to an Irish officer or pharmacist.
  • Up to 3 months' supply. Beyond that, register with a GP in Limerick.
  • Controlled medication — ADHD tablets, codeine, tramadol, benzodiazepines — needs nothing extra for Ireland. The four rules above are enough. Check the drug on HPRA if unsure.
  • Cannabis and CBD cannot enter Ireland, prescribed or not.
  • Basic kit worth packing: paracetamol, antacid, ORS, plasters, antiseptic.
Two medicine myths You do not need a licence from the Minister for Health, and there is no "HPRA personal licence". Those rules cover pharmacies importing in bulk, and travellers from inside Europe — not you. The real risk is the Gulf stopover: the UAE wants prior approval for the same drugs and enforces it with arrests. Airside transit is usually fine, an overnight is not.

Cabin bag — the document set

Immigration happens before you see your checked bags, so none of this can be in the hold. Print it — do not rely on a phone at the desk.

  • Passport with the Long Stay (D) visa sticker · visa decision letter · UL offer letter
  • Tuition payment receipts · medical insurance policy · Kilmurry accommodation confirmation
  • Proof of funds (stamped bank statements) · sponsorship letters and FSF copy · notarised affidavits
  • English language certificate · all transcripts and degree certificates · employment letters
  • Printed itinerary and boarding passes, PNR saved offline
  • 8–10 passport photos, Irish spec 35×45mm — needed for IRP, student card, bank account and PPS number
  • A full photocopy set, plus cloud backup and a USB drive
Kit worth remembering Type G plug adapters (Ireland is 230V like India — adapters only, no converter) · warm fleece, since Limerick in September is 10–15°C and wet · one full change of clothes against a delayed bag · a pen for landing cards · empty water bottle to fill after security.
Two things worth double-checking yourselfPacking lists circulating among students often say India–Dublin routes give "2 × 23 kg" — verify against your own fare, because Gulf carriers use weight concept instead. They also tend to say book the immigration appointment at Burgh Quay, Dublin. That office serves Dublin residents; if you are living in Limerick you register at Henry Street Garda Station, and UL's International Education Division arranges the appointment for you during Orientation Week.

Phone and connectivity

Two phases. A travel eSIM gets you off the plane and onto Maps; an Irish number is what the bank, the Garda station and every delivery driver actually need. Modern iPhones and most recent Androids hold both at once, so you can keep your home number live for OTPs.

Install before you fly, while you still have WiFi. Airalo and Saily are the cheap picks for a few GB; Ubigi does unlimited with uncapped tethering. No Irish number, so it is a stopgap, not a plan.

The student offer: 100GB, 5000 minutes and texts, 17GB EU roaming, no contract, on the Three network. Needs a college email, so it is a Welcome Week job rather than an arrival-day one.
No student email
GoMo€12.99/mo
Unlimited calls, texts and data on the Eir network, 30-day rolling, price held for life. Pricing. Wants an Irish address and an Irish-billable card — a Revolut card usually clears it, so open Revolut before you fly.
Guaranteed eSIM
Three or Vodafonefrom €20/mo
Dearer but certain, and activated by QR the same day. Three prepay Super Surfer is €20 unlimited data; Vodafone supports eSIM on both pay as you go and bill pay. Coverage around campus and Castletroy is fine on all of them.
Check eSIM before you commit The cheap networks are the ones least likely to support eSIM — comparison sites claim 48 and GoMo do, their own community forums say otherwise, and it keeps changing. Ask on live chat before you pay. It is rarely fatal: only the iPhone Air is eSIM-only worldwide, and iPhone 17 models sold outside the US — including Indian and European ones — still have a physical SIM tray, so a plastic SIM in the tray plus a travel eSIM works.

Budget for
What campus rent hides
Off campus you pay electricity, heating, refuse, TV licence, bedding and inventory on top of rent. Booking deposit is typically one month's rent, and leases usually run a full academic year.
Rental scams No genuine landlord asks for full rent before you arrive in Ireland. Inspect in person or have someone do it for you, insist on a written contract, and never transfer money to a listing you cannot verify. UL does not accept liability for anything on the off-campus database. Contract worries to offcampus.accomm@ul.ie.
